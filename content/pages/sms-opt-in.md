Golden Wings sends optional text messages about public screenings and premieres. That is the only topic. We do not send marketing blasts, fundraising asks, or daily chatter.

Cadence: once a month at most, and only when there is a real screening or premiere to announce.

By opting in you agree to receive automated messages from Golden Wings at the number you provide. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

- Text STOP to cancel any time.
- Text HELP for help, or email info@golden-wings-robyn.com.
- We do not sell your number. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) and [Terms of Use](/terms-of-use).

SMS delivery provider is not connected yet. Until Twilio (or another provider) is wired, opt-in numbers are forwarded by email to the production inbox only. Nothing else is stored on this server.
